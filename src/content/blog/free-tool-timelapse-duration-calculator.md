## How Long Does Your Timelapse Capture?
Maybe you forgot, or maybe you never checked. Now you have a short video and you want to know how long you really recorded for. Well you're in luck because all you need to do is use this free tool ⬇️.

## Example
A 10-second timelapse at 30FPS with a 2-second interval is:

- 300 frames
- 600 seconds recorded in real time (10 minutes)

## The Formula
It's simple really
> Real Time = Video Length x FPS x Interval

## Small FAQ
***How long should my timelapse be?***
Most are ~5-15 seconds, but it really depends on the subject.

***What FPS should I use?***
24 or 30FPS is standard.

***What interval should I use?***
- For fast movement: 1-3 seconds
- For slow movement: 5 seconds+

But if you can't remember any of these I'd recommend recording a timelapse with [Tau](https://www.trytau.app). You won't need to remember any of these details with Tau.
